    Why 13

    Scent routes directly to the limbic system, the seat of emotion and long-term memory.

    Identity forms fastest between 13 and 18. Psychologists have known this for decades, and banks have used it for just as long. The first account at 13 tends to be the account for life.

    Fragrance works the same way, only harder. The scent you pick at 13 doesn't just get remembered, it gets attached to who you were becoming. That's why we're starting here, and why we're being careful about what we say while we do it.
