# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Teenage boys in the UK, 13–17 — mobile-first, TikTok/Snapchat-native, quick to
detect and punish try-hard marketing. Secondary: their parents ("the backers"),
who often complete purchases. Visitors arrive from a shared link or word of
mouth knowing nothing; the site's one job is to convert curiosity into a
join + a vote.

## Product Purpose

Pre-launch site for INVI, a fragrance-forward body-care brand for teen boys
(from *invictus*, unconquered). Product launches later in 2026; until then the
brand deliberately shows **no product, no prices, no shop**. The site captures
a waitlist (name, email, optional age, explicit consent) and one locked vote
between three "moment" scents — ORIGIN / RISE / AFTER DARK — which segments
the list for launch. Success = joins, votes, and shares, in that order.

## Brand Personality

Confident · Together · Grounded (the brand's own tone-of-voice triad).
Short, punchy, self-assured; conviction, not bravado. Affirming, never hype —
"we back them, we don't gas them up." Speaks to boys as the young men they're
becoming. Mystery comes from withholding the product, not from vagueness about
the belief.

## Anti-references

- Lynx/Axe-era lad marketing; anything that gasses up or negs its audience.
- Deficit-model grooming ads ("fix yourself").
- Generic AI landing-page grammar: centred hero + eyebrow-label scaffolding,
  purple-gradient SaaS look, glassmorphism as decoration.
- "Coming soon" splash pages with an email field and nothing to do.

## Design Principles

1. **Cryptic about the what, clear about the why.** Product stays hidden;
   the belief is stated plainly.
2. **The visitor acts, not reads.** Join → vote → share is the spine; every
   section serves it.
3. **Route 2 "Everyday Epic".** Near-black, cinematic, blue-hour atmosphere;
   gradients are the brand's signature visual; the wordmark is embossed into
   surfaces, not printed on top.
4. **Their choice visibly matters.** Live counts, a locked vote, a numbered
   pass — co-creation is the moat.
5. **Official copy only.** Headlines come from the brand strategy deck, not
   invented catchphrases.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience includes minors: consent language is explicit, data collection is
minimal, no dark patterns. Keyboard-completable flows, visible focus,
`prefers-reduced-motion` honoured, WCAG AA contrast targets on text.
